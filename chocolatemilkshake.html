2.CHOCOLATE MILK SHAKE

Description: A delicious and healthy chocolate milk shake made with fresh milk, chocolate syrup, and vanilla ice cream.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups fresh milk
  • 1/2 cup chocolate syrup
  • 1 cup vanilla ice cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Combine all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Adjust thickness with additional milk if needed.
  3. Transfer to glasses and serve immediately.
