1.MASALA DOSA

Description: Masala dosa is a popular South Indian dish made from a crispy fermented rice-and-lentil crepe filled with spiced potato masala. It is usually served with chutney and sambar.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups dosa batter
  • 3 medium potatoes
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 green chilli, chopped
  • ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Curry leaves
  • Water as needed

Steps:

  1. Boil the potatoes
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds, curry leaves, and chopped green chilli
  3. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown
  4. Add the boiled potatoes and mix well
  5. Cook for a few more minutes and remove from heat
