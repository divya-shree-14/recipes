3.VEG FRIED RICE

Description: A delicious and healthy vegetable fried rice made with steamed rice, mixed vegetables, and a blend of spices.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups steamed rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans)
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for a minute.
  3. Add mixed vegetables and cook until tender.
  4. Add steamed rice and all the spices. Mix well.
  5. Cook for a few more minutes and serve hot.
